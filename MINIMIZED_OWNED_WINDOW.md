## Ownership and lifetime

Minimize requires a healthy observed-active scene. Restore and all controls honor existing main-thread/running/closing/closePending checks and lifetime decisions. Both transitions operate on the same session/window/controller/workspace; neither calls prepareScene, start/keep, private activation, reparenting or native geometry setters. The15-second deadline and heartbeat schedule do not reset. Explicit Keep may precede minimize. Lock/data/host/scene failure still closes, and stop is terminal for presentation state. If clipping causes UIKit/client scene inactivity, the unchanged policy closes instead of overriding that safety condition.

Closing a bar follows the same owned cleanup and replacement proof as closing an expanded window. The menu/restore controls are released during confirmed UI cleanup. Earlier private owner/admission/lock/lifetime/helper/package lifecycle source is unchanged. Metadata freshness is checked at original preparation; restore does not treat that historical descriptor as a new admission request.
