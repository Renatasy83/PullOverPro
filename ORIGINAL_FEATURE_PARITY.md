# Original feature coverage

Reference: GPL-3.0 `c1d3rdev/PullOver-Pro`, upstream `4a227b75668929894d7faa5a83ea4bf2ba81edc0`, especially `PullOverViewController.m`, `POHandle.m`, `QuickSwitchTableView` and the preferences controllers. This is a feature map, not complete compatibility confirmation. Existing attribution and full license remain in the package.

RC1 has a user-confirmed Safari kept/manual-close baseline. RC2 rebuilds the compact original-style switcher/headerless panel and adds guarded source-independent construction. See [CLASSIC_UI_RC2.md](CLASSIC_UI_RC2.md) and the versioned validation report. RC2 now has three source-independent Safari scene/layer/cleanup sessions with sampled classic geometry and direct user confirmation of visible page, navigation, keyboard and X. This covers the stated Safari gate; it is not a terminated-process, all-controls or every-app claim.
